The movement

Twenty‑five jewels, and a rotor signed by hand

25Jewels, engraved on the caseback
316LGerman stainless steel
2Sapphire crystals — front and back
1:27Minutes a day a 99.9%‑accurate watch drifts

Components come from a small network of specialists in Switzerland and Germany, many of whom have spent generations perfecting a single part. They return to Reykjavík to be assembled, regulated and inspected here — which is why the back of the case is glass.

The bench is the factory

Behind the shopfront on Laugavegur there is a workshop, and that is the whole manufacture. Ideas are drawn, refined and tested here. Parts arrive from Switzerland and Germany. Every watch is put together, timed and checked in this room before it leaves the country.

Visitors are welcome. It is an owner-operated workshop, showroom and design studio.
